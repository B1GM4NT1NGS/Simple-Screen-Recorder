# Privacy policy

Simple Screen Recorder records the screen area and audio sources selected by the user. Recordings, temporary footage, and application settings are stored locally on the user's computer. The application does not upload recordings, send telemetry, collect analytics, or use a remote recording service.

The application enumerates connected displays and Windows audio devices locally to provide the capture and audio selectors. The user chooses the recording area, audio sources, and save location. The microphone is muted by default.

Clicking the GitHub or Buy me a coffee button opens the requested website in the user's browser. Those websites handle visits under their own privacy policies: [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and [Buy me a coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/privacy-policy). The recorder does not send recordings or settings to either service.

Running from source requires downloading dependencies during initial setup. The standalone executable does not require that setup. See [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY.md) for bundled components.

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/B1GM4NT1NGS/Simple-Screen-Recorder).
